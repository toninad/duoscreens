Privacy.

Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version: your screenshots stay on your device unless you turn on sync (Pro), and then they're end-to-end encrypted, so I can't see them. I collect as little as possible, and nothing is used for ads or tracking.

HearHere Studio is run by Toni Nad, who is responsible for your data. Contact: feedback@hearhere.media.

Your screenshots and texts

Everything you make is processed and saved in your browser. Screenshots, headlines and exports aren't uploaded to any server, unless you turn on sync (below).

The only exception is translation (Pro). When you translate, the headline and subheadline texts, and the languages you picked, are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be translated. Screenshots are never sent. Anthropic processes the text to return the translation; see Anthropic's privacy policy.

Sync (Pro, optional)

If you turn on “Sync across browsers”, your boards (screenshots, texts, settings and board names) are stored on the HearHere Studio server, hosted on Cloudflare, so your other browsers with the same license key can get them. Sync is off until you turn it on.

  • Everything is end-to-end encrypted in your browser before it's uploaded, with keys made from your license key. Your license key is sent to check your license, as with translation, but the server keeps only a fingerprint of it and never keeps the encryption keys, so what it stores can't be read.
  • What the server can see: how many boards and images you have, their sizes, when they changed, and a fingerprint of your license key.
  • Images no board uses any more are deleted automatically after 7 days. Deleted boards leave a small marker for 90 days so your other browsers know to delete them too.
  • Delete synced data in the board menu removes everything synced for your key immediately. If Pro is refunded or revoked, synced data is deleted 30 days later.
  • Anyone who has your license key can read your synced boards, so keep the key to yourself.

Buying Pro

Payments are handled by Polar, the merchant of record. Polar collects your name, email, billing country and payment details to process the purchase and VAT; see Polar's privacy policy at polar.sh. I never see your card details.

Your license key

To check that a license is valid, the HearHere Studio server keeps:

  • a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your license key, never the key itself, and its status from Polar;
  • a random ID your browser makes up once, to count how many browsers use the key (up to 3);
  • how many translations the key has used this month.

None of this contains your name or email.

Technical data

To stop abuse, the server counts requests per IP address. That counter is deleted after about two minutes. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (like IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your boards (screenshots, texts and settings), your license key, the random browser ID and your device choices, so your work is there next time. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads. You can delete boards from the board menu, remove the license under Pro, or clear the site's data in your browser. Some browsers, Safari in particular, may clear a site's storage if you don't visit for a while: use Export backup, or sync, to keep a copy.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to your data protection authority. Email feedback@hearhere.media with your license key, and I'll remove its records.
