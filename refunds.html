Refunds.

Last updated 24 October 2026

If HearHere Studio Pro isn't right for you, you get your money back: a full refund within 14 days of buying, no questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

Email feedback@hearhere.media from the address you bought with, or include your license key or order number. You can also reply to your Polar receipt.

What happens next

  • The refund goes back to your original payment method through Polar, our merchant of record, usually within a few business days, depending on your bank.
  • Your license key is deactivated, and exports carry the watermark again.

After 14 days

If something isn't working, write anyway. I'll try to fix it, and I'll look at refunds after 14 days case by case. This doesn't affect any rights you have under consumer law.
